How to use it

  1. Charge the sealer with the USB cable included.
  2. Fill your bag and press the open end flat.
  3. Hold the sealer across the bag opening and press the one-touch button.
  4. Release when the seal is done and let it cool a moment.
  5. To open a bag, use the opening function of the tool.

Use only on suitable plastic bags. Test on one bag first. Follow the manufacturer's safety instructions that come in the box.
